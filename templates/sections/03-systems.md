## 2. Systems

Systems discovered via REST (`lssystem`). Multiple hosts in one document may be a **Flash Grid**, a **partnership pair**, or simply **independent systems** at the same site — see Overview for auto-classification.

The **Flash Grid** column is filled only when that array reports a grid name (not invented by the tool).
